Goalies

Enter these the same way as the score sheet: list each team's goalies in the order they played, with the period and time each one came out. Leave the time blank for the goalie who finished the game. If a goalie went back in later, add him again on a new row. GA is worked out from the goal times -- a goalie is charged with every goal up to and including the time he came out -- so you can check it against the Goals box on the sheet.

On Ice

Check off everyone who stepped on the ice for this game -- same as the checkmarks on the paper game sheet. Shown on the public schedule once the game's saved.
