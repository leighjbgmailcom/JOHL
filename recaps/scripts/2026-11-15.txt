Arnie Jordan here, coming to you from row three at Jordan Arena, with the Rinkside Report. Sunday, November fifteenth. Last week my nephew said we try crayons, so I came with a coffee, the notebook, and the big box. Sixty-four colours! Thirty-seven goals. You'd think that's plenty. You would be wrong.

The early game, the J.E. Culp Habs against the Timbit Senators. Dixon opened it, Williams tied it, and Tooby put the Habs back up. Then, with two seconds left in the first, Greg Penney scored all by himself. Two seconds. That's not a goal, that's a doorbell ditch.

Then the Habs went to the buffet and didn't come back. Kay Pee, thirty-six seconds into the second. Holt got one back. Cooke. Deakin. Then Greg Penney, Tooby and Dixon, three goals in three minutes, and Chinner made it nine to three. Deakin got his second late. By then I'd used red, blue, white, a second red, and Burnt Sienna. I don't know what Burnt Sienna is. Buynink came out of the Senators net for Shannon halfway through, and the Habs said welcome, here's six more.

In the third, Williams and Simon Murphy made it nine to six. Then Hunt, and Ort. Eleven to six, final. Tooby had five points, Dixon and Greg Penney four each. Williams had two goals and two assists, a lovely night as long as you don't look up. And Ort scored, set one up, and took three for roughing at the exact same second Schmidt took three for slashing. Those two weren't battling, they were synchronizing watches.

Game two, The Keg Canucks against Dawson's Oilers. Adam Short and Beckett put the Canucks up two nothing. Then the second period became the McPherson Show. McPherson from Mansion. McPherson again, tie game, and on that same second both teams changed goalies.

Snihur went off for tripping, and the Canucks scored anyway. Koke, short-handed, from Pundy and Goodie. McPherson got it back on the power play, three all. Then Hound Dog sat three for too many men. Hound Dog, I counted, and the Oilers had plenty of fellas out there. That was the problem. Colin Murphy scored right after it ran out, from Adam Short and Wes Short. The Short to Short connection now comes with a Murphy extension.

McPherson tied it four all in the third. Four goals, every Oiler goal, and Mansion helped on all four. That's not a line, that's a two-man band. Then New Guy scored, and a minute later New Guy scored again. Six to four Canucks. McPherson, four goals and a loss. That's catching four fish and the boat sinks. Goodie had four assists, and New Guy had three points, so fellas, he's not that new anymore. And Dawson gave up four in front of a team named after him. Nobody respects the landlord. Late in it, Mansion and Adam Short went off together for roughing. Mansion couldn't find the puck, so he settled for Short.

The late game, the ColdBreak Leafs against the RegattaSport Nordiques. Pierce scored twenty-seven seconds in. Brophy went off for hooking, and Lawrence scored short-handed anyway. Two short-handed goals in one night. I don't own a crayon for that. Then Pierce got the power-play goal, from Steele. Two to one Leafs.

In the second, Collini went off for slashing, and the Nordiques made it count. Rob DeBolster scored while he sat, and thirty-five seconds later Dykstra, three to two Nordiques. Collini, one slash, two goals against. Bad exchange rate. Mallet came out for Rosie, Jay Mac came out for Trigg, and Pulo made it four to two with fifty-eight seconds left in the period.

Langlois scored early in the third. Larry Penney got one back. Then Moorzey scored from Regular and Lawrence, and on that same second, Brophy went off for roughing. Brophy, your team is up by three, and you still needed a sit-down. Six minutes in the box, most of anybody all night. Horton got one more for the Leafs, and that was it. Six to four Nordiques. Last week the Leafs won with thirty-six seconds left. This week they led after one and never saw the lead again.

Somewhere in there, a clearing attempt went so high it rang off the clock. I didn't catch who shot it, and I'm not asking. Then I set my coffee on the crayon box, and now Burnt Sienna smells like a double double.

So that's your week. Eleven for the Habs, four for McPherson in a loss, and a New Guy who scored two and is still the New Guy. By the late game I was writing Nordiques goals in Mauve. My nephew says next week, a whiteboard. If I got your name wrong, or gave your goal to the fella beside you, take it up with the refs, the timekeeper, and how fast you guys skate out there. Tape your sticks. This has been Arnie Jordan with the Rinkside Report.
